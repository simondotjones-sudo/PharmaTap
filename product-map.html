A thoughtful foundation.

The proposed PharmaTap product, built around Stacks.

The idea

Less searching.
More following through.

A pharmacy quality and operations platform connecting procedures, daily routines, reporting, actions and learning.

For the colleague

One clear next step

Find a procedure, record a check, report a concern or complete a short refresher.

Ask in everyday language

Search approved SOPs with source references, or turn a typed/spoken account into a draft report for confirmation.

Safe handover

Patient care takes priority. Medication incidents go to the appropriate pharmacist for assessment and follow-up.

For the group

Consistent, with local applicability

Group SOPs, controlled local variations, site assignments and individual staff acknowledgements.

Evidence behind every action

Assigned owners, deadlines, completion evidence, pharmacist review and an effectiveness check.

Learn across twenty pharmacies

Share anonymised incident learning and track the resulting procedure changes and training.

Build it in deliberate stages.

01 · First release

Knowledge + reporting

Approved SOP library and cited search. Errors, near misses, maintenance, shared actions. Role-based access and audit history.

02 · Daily operations

Checks + learning

Schedules, exception workflows, assets, calibration, acknowledgements, induction and short refreshers.

03 · Group assurance

Oversight + evidence

Recalls, internal audits, inspection packs, cross-site learning and agreed integrations.

Roles and responsibilities

RoleProposed access
Staff / technicianAssigned-site checks, reporting, SOPs and learning; no clinical incident sign-off.
Pharmacy managerOperational assignments and maintenance; clinical permissions only if separately authorised.
Supervising pharmacistSite incident review, SOP implementation, corrective actions and clinical sign-off.
Superintendent / group governanceGroup scope, SOP approval, controlled local variations and governance reporting.
ContractorOnly assigned maintenance work and evidence; no patient safety records.

The demo simplifies these into Staff, Pharmacist and Group admin views. Production roles must be configured and enforced on the server.

From demo to a live service

Shared, secure records

Authenticated named users, organisation/site scope, controlled patient information, protected attachments, retention rules, append-only audit history and tested backups.

Controlled knowledge

Import the client’s SOPs, confirm ownership and approval, extract searchable text, index only the current applicable versions, and show exact source passages. AI proposes; authorised people approve.

Dependable operations

Scheduled jobs, escalation notifications, retries, monitoring, accessible mobile flows, duplicate protection and server-calculated reporting definitions.

Existing clinical systems

Keep dispensing and clinical records in their designated systems. Integrations and any statutory registers are separate scoped work; this demo does not replace them.

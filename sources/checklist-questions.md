# Checklist questions

Every checklist in the schedule has its questions here, grouped by frequency and in the same owner order. Each question is phrased so that Yes is the compliant answer. Questions marked (record) capture a value, name or date instead.

## Daily

### Fridge Temperature Log

Owner: Staff

1. (record) Current, minimum and maximum temperature for each medicines fridge.
2. Were all three readings between 2°C and 8°C?
3. Was the thermometer reset after the readings were taken?
4. Is the fridge free of food, drink and other non-medicinal items?
5. Is stock spaced so air can circulate, with the door closing fully?
6. If any reading was out of range, was the pharmacist told at once and the action recorded?

### Dispensary Room Temperature Log

Owner: Staff

1. (record) Current, minimum and maximum temperature for the dispensary and each other medicines storage area.
2. Were all readings at or below 25°C?
3. Was the thermometer reset after the readings were taken?
4. Are medicines stored off the floor and away from direct sunlight and heat sources?
5. If any reading was out of range, was the pharmacist told at once and the action recorded?

### Near Miss and Dispensing Error Log

Owner: Staff

1. (record) Number of near misses logged today. Enter 0 if none.
2. Was each near miss recorded at the time it was caught?
3. Does each entry say what went wrong and the likely contributing factor?
4. Was any error that reached a patient reported to the pharmacist on duty straight away?
5. Has the pharmacist on duty seen today's entries?

### Closing and Security Check

Owner: Staff

1. Is the controlled drugs safe locked, with keys held as the key-holding policy requires?
2. Are all prescription medicines, including bags awaiting collection, secured in the dispensary?
3. Are the fridge doors closed and the fridges running?
4. Are prescriptions and patient records put away and computers logged off?
5. Is confidential waste secured?
6. Are the doors and shutters locked and the alarm set?

### Opening and Pharmacist Presence Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Was a registered pharmacist on the premises before any medicine was sold or supplied?
2. Are the current registration certificates for the pharmacy and the supervising pharmacist on public display?
3. Is staffing sufficient for today's expected workload?
4. Have handover notes from the previous shift been read?
5. Are the dispensing system, label printer and interaction alerts working?
6. Is the patient consultation area clean and available?

### Duty Register Sign-In

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Has each pharmacist on duty today entered their name and PSI registration number?
2. Are arrival and departure times recorded for each pharmacist?
3. Has each pharmacist signed their entry?
4. If a pharmaceutical assistant covered a temporary absence, is that period recorded?
5. Was a pharmacist present for all of today's opening hours, with no gap in the register?

### Controlled Drugs Register Entries and Safe Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Has every Schedule 2 receipt and supply today been entered in the register, today or by tomorrow at the latest?
2. Does each supply entry show the date, patient name and address, prescriber and quantity?
3. Does each receipt entry show the supplier's name and address and the quantity?
4. Is the running balance updated for every entry?
5. Were corrections made only by a dated, signed marginal note or footnote?
6. Was every controlled drug prescription checked for validity before supply?
7. Are all Schedule 2 and 3 controlled drugs locked in the safe, with the key under the pharmacist's control?

### Daily Prescription Register Sign-Off

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Has today's prescription register (daily audit report) been printed?
2. Has a pharmacist signed and dated it today or within 24 hours?
3. Do the entries match today's prescriptions for patient, prescriber, product, quantity and prescription date?
4. Is each emergency supply recorded with its reason?
5. Have today's dispensed prescriptions been filed for retention?
6. Is the report filed with the registers for the previous two years?

### Medicine Authentication (FMD) Alerts Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Was every pack carrying a 2D barcode scanned and decommissioned at supply?
2. Was the anti-tamper seal checked on each of those packs?
3. Was every alert investigated before the pack was supplied?
4. Were packs with unresolved alerts quarantined away from live stock?
5. Were unresolved alerts reported as the pharmacy's procedure requires?

## Weekly

### Owings and Uncollected Prescriptions Review

Owner: Staff

1. Has every outstanding owing been checked against stock received this week?
2. Is every owing older than one week either ordered, supplied or followed up with the patient?
3. Have bags uncollected beyond the pharmacy's time limit been referred to the pharmacist?
4. Have fridge items and controlled drugs awaiting collection been checked separately?
5. Were items returned to stock only on the pharmacist's decision, with patient labels removed?

### Patient-Returned and Waste Medicines Check

Owner: Staff

1. Are patient-returned medicines kept in the designated area, separate from live stock?
2. Are expired and damaged medicines segregated from live stock?
3. Have patient details been removed from or obscured on returned packs?
4. Have returned controlled drugs been handed to the pharmacist?
5. Are the medicines waste and sharps bins closed and below the fill line?
6. (record) Date of the last waste collection.

### Dispensary Cleaning and Hygiene Check

Owner: Staff

1. Were benches, the sink and dispensing equipment cleaned as the cleaning schedule requires?
2. Is the fridge interior clean and free of spills and ice build-up?
3. Are shelves and floors in all storage areas clean and uncluttered?
4. Does the dispensary sink have hot and cold water, soap and hand-drying supplies?
5. Are tablet counters and measures cleaned after use?
6. Are staff toilet and hand-washing facilities clean and stocked?
7. Has the cleaning record been signed for this week?

### Fire Alarm and Exit Routes Check

Owner: Staff

1. Was the fire alarm tested this week and heard throughout the premises?
2. (record) Call point used for the test.
3. Are all exit routes and fire doors clear and unlocked during opening hours?
4. Are exit signs visible and lit?
5. Are extinguishers in place and unobstructed?
6. Has the test been entered in the fire register?

### Controlled Drugs Running Balance Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. (record) Schedule 2 products checked this week.
2. Does the physical stock in the safe match the register balance for each product checked?
3. For methadone and other liquids, was the volume measured and any overage accounted for?
4. Were dispensed items awaiting collection and expired stock included in the count?
5. Was the check recorded in the register, dated and signed by the pharmacist?
6. If a discrepancy was found, was it investigated, recorded and reported to the supervising pharmacist?

### Opioid Substitution Treatment Records Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Is there a valid, current prescription for every patient dosed this week?
2. Is every supervised and take-away dose this week recorded?
3. Were missed doses recorded, and the prescriber contacted where the procedure requires it?
4. Do the controlled drugs register entries match the doses supplied?
5. Are pre-poured doses labelled with the patient's name and stored in the safe?
6. Were take-away doses supplied in child-resistant containers?

### Recalls and Safety Notices Review

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Have all HPRA recalls and safety notices received this week been read?
2. Has stock been checked against every recalled batch?
3. Has affected stock been quarantined and returned as the notice instructs?
4. For patient-level recalls, have affected patients been identified and contacted?
5. Is the action taken on each notice recorded and signed?
6. Have relevant notices and PSI alerts been shared with the team?

### Duty Register Review and Sign-Off

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Is there an entry for every day the pharmacy opened this week?
2. Is each entry complete, with name, registration number, times and signature?
3. Do the entries cover all opening hours without a gap?
4. Was each locum's registration verified on the PSI register before they worked?
5. Is any pharmaceutical assistant cover recorded and within the permitted limits?
6. Has the supervising pharmacist signed and dated this week's review?

## Monthly

### Expiry Date and Stock Rotation Check

Owner: Staff

1. (record) Areas or shelf sections checked this month.
2. Is every storage area on schedule within the check cycle, including the fridge, medicines counter, storerooms and controlled drugs safe?
3. Have items inside the pharmacy's short-date window been marked?
4. Have expired items been removed from live stock to the segregated area?
5. Is the shortest-dated stock placed to be used first?
6. Have expired controlled drugs been passed to the pharmacist?
7. Is the check recorded, dated and signed?

### PCRS Claims Submission and Reconciliation

Owner: Staff

1. Were all claims for the month submitted to the PCRS by the deadline?
2. Do the claim totals for each scheme match the dispensing system's records?
3. Have last month's rejected claims been corrected and resubmitted?
4. Are the prescriptions and supporting documents for claims filed and retrievable?
5. Has the payment listing been reconciled against the claims submitted?
6. Have unexplained differences been raised with the pharmacist or owner?

### High-Tech Medicines Reconciliation

Owner: Staff

1. Does the physical high-tech stock match the records for each patient?
2. Was each item ordered against a valid prescription for a named patient?
3. Are high-tech medicines stored separately from other stock and identified by patient?
4. Were fridge-stored high-tech items within range all month?
5. Has stock for patients who stopped treatment been dealt with as the HSE procedure requires?
6. Are all orders and receipts recorded on the HSE high-tech ordering system?
7. Have discrepancies been reported to the pharmacist?

### First Aid, Emergency Lighting and Extinguisher Check

Owner: Staff

1. Is the first aid kit fully stocked with in-date items?
2. Was the emergency lighting tested and working?
3. Is each extinguisher in place, with its gauge in the green and its pin and seal intact?
4. Is the service label on each extinguisher in date?
5. Is the accident record available and up to date?
6. Are the results entered in the fire register?

### Full Controlled Drugs Stock Reconciliation

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Has every Schedule 2 product in the safe been counted and compared with its register balance?
2. Do all balances match?
3. Were this month's entries made on the day or the next day, in date order and in full?
4. Do supplier invoices and requisitions match the receipts entered?
5. Is expired stock marked, segregated in the safe and still included in the register balance?
6. Are patient-returned controlled drugs kept separate from stock and recorded outside the register?
7. Is the reconciliation recorded, dated and signed by the pharmacist?
8. Was any discrepancy investigated and reported to the supervising and superintendent pharmacists?

### Anaphylaxis Kit and Vaccination Supplies Check

Owner: Pharmacist

1. Does the anaphylaxis kit hold adrenaline in the quantities the vaccination protocol requires?
2. Are all items in the kit in date?
3. Is the kit within immediate reach of the vaccination area?
4. Were vaccines stored between 2°C and 8°C all month?
5. Is a sharps bin available and below the fill line?
6. Are needles, syringes, swabs and gloves in sufficient supply?
7. Have all vaccinations given this month been recorded and reported as required?

### Near Miss and Error Trend Review

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. (record) Number of near misses and number of errors that reached a patient this month.
2. Has the supervising pharmacist reviewed every record for the month?
3. Does each error record show contributing factors, corrective action and preventative action?
4. Have all identified actions been completed?
5. Were errors reported to the superintendent pharmacist?
6. Have the records been checked for recurring patterns, such as look-alike products or busy periods?
7. Have the lessons been shared with the whole team?
8. Has any SOP affected by this month's findings been flagged for update?

### Staff Training Records Check

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Is there a training record for every staff member involved in selling or supplying medicines?
2. Has each staff member signed the SOPs relevant to their role?
3. Have this month's new starters completed induction, including confidentiality and when to refer to the pharmacist?
4. Was training due this month completed and recorded?
5. Has staff competence been checked by observation or questioning?
6. Are the records available on the premises?

## Quarterly

### Premises and Consultation Area Inspection

Owner: Staff

1. Are all medicines stored inside the registered premises, in designated areas and off the floor?
2. Are pharmacy-only medicines kept where the public cannot reach them?
3. Are the public, dispensary and storage areas clean, well maintained and professionally presented?
4. Are dispensary benches uncluttered, with clear space for labelling and pharmacist checking?
5. Is the consultation area clean, private and free of stored stock?
6. Are patients told they can ask for a private consultation?
7. Is the premises accessible for wheelchair users and people with mobility or sight difficulties?
8. Can the registration certificates be read from the public area, and are they current?
9. Have defects such as damp, poor lighting or poor ventilation been reported?

### Equipment Calibration and Maintenance Check

Owner: Staff

1. Is the electronic balance working, with its calibration in date?
2. Are the certified weights present and maintained?
3. Are the glass measures and compounding equipment present, clean and undamaged?
4. Are all thermometers working, with calibration in date?
5. Are the dispensing computers, label printers and scanners working, with backups running?
6. Are child-resistant closures and dispensing containers in stock?
7. Are up-to-date reference sources accessible to the pharmacist?
8. Have faults been logged and repairs arranged?

### Health and Safety Walk-Through

Owner: Staff

1. Are floors, stairs and walkways free of slip and trip hazards?
2. Is stock stored safely, with heavy items at a safe height and steps in good condition?
3. Are cables, plugs and sockets undamaged and not overloaded?
4. Are sharps and hazardous waste handled as the procedure requires?
5. Do staff know the lone working and robbery procedures?
6. Have this quarter's accidents and near accidents been recorded and followed up?
7. Can staff access the safety statement?
8. Have hazards found today been reported to the owner?

### SOP Compliance Spot Audit

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. (record) SOP selected, its version number and implementation date.
2. Was the process observed being carried out?
3. Did the observed practice match the SOP?
4. Have any deviations been recorded and added to an action plan?
5. Have the staff carrying out the process been trained on the current version?
6. Is the SOP within its review date?
7. Has the superintendent pharmacist approved the current version?

### Controlled Drugs Destruction Records Check

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Is all expired Schedule 2 stock marked and segregated in the safe?
2. Has witnessed destruction been requested for the expired Schedule 2 stock held?
3. Was every destruction this quarter witnessed by an authorised person?
4. Does the register show the date, quantity and witness for each destruction?
5. Were patient-returned controlled drugs destroyed and recorded separately from pharmacy stock?
6. Are destruction records for the last two years available at the pharmacy?

### Delivery Service Check

Owner: Supervising pharmacist. Complete only if the pharmacy delivers.

1. Does the pharmacist assess whether each delivery request is appropriate?
2. Does the pharmacist confirm that counselling can be achieved without face-to-face contact?
3. Is a full therapeutic review done every time a prescription is delivered?
4. Do patients receive enough information on use, storage and disposal?
5. Is the delivery method secure and prompt, with access limited to authorised people?
6. Does the method protect the medicines in transit, including fridge items?
7. Is there an itemised audit trail from the pharmacy to a signature on receipt?
8. Are failed deliveries handled as the procedure requires?

### Confidentiality and Confidential Waste Check

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Is a current confidentiality policy in place?
2. Have all staff been trained in it, with records kept?
3. Can conversations at the counter and in the consultation area be held without being overheard?
4. Are screens, prescriptions and bagged medicines out of public view?
5. Is confidential waste shredded or collected by a secure contractor?
6. Are patient labels removed from returned medicines before disposal?
7. Was any breach this quarter recorded and reported to the owner?

### Non-Prescription Controlled Sales Audit

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. (record) Number of sales observed. PSI's self-assessment uses five.
2. Was a pharmacist present and supervising each sale?
3. Did staff ask who the medicine was for, the symptoms, their duration and other medicines taken?
4. Was the pharmacist personally involved in every codeine sale?
5. Was codeine supplied only where non-codeine pain relief had been tried or was unsuitable?
6. Was each codeine patient advised on short-term use and the risk of dependence?
7. Are codeine products stored out of the public's sight and reach?
8. Were repeat or multiple-pack requests for pseudoephedrine and similar products referred to the pharmacist?
9. Have all staff been trained on the codeine SOP, with records kept?

### Complaints and Incidents Review

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. (record) Number of complaints and incidents this quarter.
2. Is each one recorded with the date, details and action taken?
3. Did each complainant receive a response within the pharmacy's stated time?
4. Were serious incidents reported to the superintendent pharmacist and owner?
5. Have all agreed actions been completed?
6. Have the records been checked for recurring causes?
7. Have the lessons been shared with the team?

### Data Protection and System Access Review

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Does every user have their own login to the dispensing system?
2. Have accounts for staff who left this quarter been removed?
3. Do access levels match each person's role?
4. Are backups running, and has a restore been tested?
5. Is CCTV signage displayed and footage kept only for the stated period?
6. Were data access requests answered within one month?
7. Was any data breach recorded and, where required, reported to the Data Protection Commission within 72 hours?
8. Are contracts in place with the software, shredding and CCTV suppliers?

## Annually

### Fire Drill (twice a year)

Owner: Staff

1. Was a full evacuation drill carried out?
2. (record) Date of the drill and time taken to evacuate.
3. Did all staff reach the assembly point?
4. Did staff know how to help customers leave, including anyone in the consultation area?
5. Was the dispensary left secure during the drill?
6. Is the drill recorded in the fire register, with any problems noted?
7. Has each problem been assigned to someone to fix?

### PSI Pharmacy Assessment System Self-Assessment

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Has every applicable section been completed in the last 12 months?
2. Did the supervising pharmacist complete each section with the pharmacy team?
3. Has a compliance level been selected for each section?
4. Is there an action plan for every area that fell short of compliant?
5. Does each action have an owner and a due date?
6. Have last year's actions been completed?
7. Are the completed assessment and action plans kept at the pharmacy for inspection?

### Staff Training Needs and CPD Review

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. Has each staff member had a training review this year?
2. Does each staff member have a training plan for the coming year?
3. Has every pharmacist met their CPD requirements for the year?
4. Has training been delivered on new services, SOP changes and new PSI guidance?
5. Do staffing levels and skill mix still match the services offered?

### Vaccination and Clinical Service Training Renewal

Owner: Supervising pharmacist

1. (record) Services offered that need specific training.
2. Does each vaccinating pharmacist hold in-date vaccination, CPR and anaphylaxis training?
3. Are the training certificates on file?
4. Do the service procedures reflect this year's programme and current guidance?
5. Does the consultation area still meet the requirements for vaccination?
6. Are pharmacists trained to current guidance for each other clinical service offered?

### PSI Pharmacy Registration Renewal

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Was the continued registration application submitted before the deadline?
2. Has the fee been paid?
3. Are the owner, superintendent and supervising pharmacist details on the register correct?
4. Were this year's changes in ownership, key personnel or premises notified to the PSI?
5. Does the floor plan held by the PSI match the current premises?
6. Is the new certificate on public display?

### Self-Assessment Action Plan Sign-Off

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Have the superintendent pharmacist and owner reviewed every completed section?
2. Have they signed each action plan?
3. Have the resources needed to complete the actions been provided?
4. (record) Support given to the supervising pharmacist during the assessment.
5. Have overdue actions been given new dates and escalated?
6. Are the signed plans kept at the pharmacy?

### SOP Full Review and Staff Sign-Off

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Are all the SOPs that PSI recommends in place, plus one for each additional service?
2. Has each SOP been reviewed this year and given a new review date?
3. Has the superintendent pharmacist approved each SOP?
4. Do the SOPs reflect this year's changes in legislation and PSI guidance?
5. Do they reflect lessons from this year's errors and near misses?
6. Are superseded versions archived under version control?
7. Have all staff read and signed the current versions relevant to their role?

### Fridge and Thermometer Servicing

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Has each pharmaceutical fridge been serviced this year?
2. Is the service report on file?
3. Have thermometers been calibrated or replaced, with certificates on file?
4. Has each fridge alarm been tested?
5. Is fridge capacity still adequate for orderly, well-spaced storage?
6. Is there a plan for fridge failure, including where stock will be moved?

### Pharmacist and Staff Registration Verification

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Has every pharmacist's registration been checked on the PSI register this year?
2. Has every pharmaceutical assistant's registration been checked?
3. Were regular locums included in the check?
4. Is the date of each check recorded?
5. Are any conditions attached to a registration known and managed?
6. Does the supervising pharmacist still meet the experience requirement and supervise only this pharmacy?
7. Are technicians' qualifications on file?

### Business Continuity Plan Review

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Is there a written plan covering loss of pharmacist cover, IT failure, power failure, fridge failure and loss of premises?
2. Has the plan been reviewed this year?
3. Are emergency contacts current for locums, the IT supplier, wholesalers, the PSI and the HSE?
4. Do staff know the temporary closure procedure, including how patients are told?
5. Does the plan cover patients on daily supervised doses during a closure?
6. If the plan was tested or used this year, were the lessons recorded?

### Contractor Review

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Is the medicines waste contractor authorised, with collection records on file?
2. Does the confidential waste contractor provide certificates of destruction?
3. Is a pest control contract in place, with visit reports on file?
4. Are the alarm, CCTV and fire system maintenance contracts current?
5. Does every contractor with access to patient data have a data processing agreement?
6. Has each contractor's performance been reviewed this year?

### Safety Statement and Risk Assessment Review

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Has the safety statement been reviewed and signed this year?
2. Do the risk assessments cover manual handling, lone working, robbery and aggression, sharps and hazardous medicines?
3. Do they reflect this year's changes in premises, services or staffing?
4. Has the safety statement been brought to every staff member's attention?
5. Have the actions arising from this year's accidents been completed?

### Fire Equipment Servicing

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Have all extinguishers been serviced by a competent contractor this year?
2. Has the fire alarm system been serviced at the required intervals?
3. Has the emergency lighting been serviced?
4. Are the service certificates filed in the fire register?
5. Has the fire risk assessment been reviewed?
6. Have all reported defects been fixed?

### Professional Indemnity and Insurance Renewal

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Is professional indemnity cover in place for the pharmacy and every pharmacist, including locums?
2. Does the cover include every service offered, such as vaccination?
3. Have employer's liability and public liability cover been renewed?
4. Do the sums insured reflect current stock values, including fridge stock?
5. Are the policy documents on file?
6. Were this year's incidents notified to the insurer as the policy requires?

### Data Retention and Records Disposal Review

Owner: Owner / Superintendent

1. Is there a written retention schedule covering prescriptions, registers, patient records, CCTV and staff records?
2. Have prescriptions and prescription registers been kept for at least two years?
3. Have controlled drugs registers been kept for at least two years from the last entry?
4. Were records past their retention period securely destroyed, with the destruction recorded?
5. Is the privacy notice current and available to patients?
6. Are archived records stored securely?
